The short version

Block Puzzle Color Blast has no accounts and no server of its own. Your data sits in three places: on your phone, in your Google Play Games profile, and, as anonymous usage and crash data, in our Google Firebase project. Each can be deleted as below.

1. Stop sending usage and crash data

  1. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: open Block Puzzle Color Blast, go to Settings → Privacy & Terms and switch Share usage data off. Nothing more is sent from then on.
  2. Elsewhere: uninstalling the app stops it.

2. Delete your progress on your phone

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Block Puzzle Color Blast → Storage.
  2. Tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Your scores, progress, settings and streak are erased at once. The app is not backed up, so no other copy exists.

3. Delete your Google Play Games data

  1. Open your Google Play Games profile (in the Play Games app, or the Play Store's profile menu).
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Block Puzzle Color Blast and tap Delete. This removes your achievements and your leaderboard scores for this game.

4. Ask us to delete usage and crash data

Email goldengarudastudios@gmail.com with the subject Block Puzzle Color Blast data deletion. We reply within 30 days and delete whatever we can link to your request.

This data carries no name or email address, only a random identifier for one installation of the app, so we may not be able to pick out your records from an email alone. Either way it does not outlive the retention periods below.

What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

  • Scores, progress, settings, streak (on your phone): deleted immediately in step 2. We never hold a copy.
  • Play Games achievements and leaderboard scores: deleted by Google when you complete step 3.
  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics): kept for at most 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Your email to us: kept only as long as needed to answer it.

Nothing else is kept. See the Privacy Policy for what each kind of data contains.
