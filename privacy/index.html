Block Puzzle Color Blast

Privacy Policy

Google Play package fudge.block.puzzle.colorblast
Published by Golden Garuda Studios

Last updated 20 October 2026.

The short version

Block Puzzle Color Blast keeps your scores, settings and current game on your device. It has no internet access, shows no ads, has no accounts and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

One file, holding only what the game needs to work:

  • your best score
  • your statistics: games played, total score, blocks placed, lines cleared, best combo, and how many days in a row you have played
  • the game you are part-way through, so you can carry on where you left off
  • your settings: vibration, colour theme, and whether shape symbols are shown on the blocks

That file is private to the app. It is not copied to any backup or server. Android removes it when you uninstall Block Puzzle Color Blast, and deleting the app's data in Android Settings erases it immediately.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising code.

Permissions

  • VIBRATE — for the small haptic taps when you place blocks and clear lines. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.

The app asks for no other permissions.

If you email us

If you send us a message, we will see your email address and whatever you wrote, and we use it only to answer you. We keep it only as long as needed to do so.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or rent any information about you.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Children

Block Puzzle Color Blast is a puzzle game for a general audience. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Block Puzzle Color Blast changes what it collects or sends — for example by adding ads — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.
