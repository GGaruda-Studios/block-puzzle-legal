Block Puzzle Color Blast

Privacy Policy

Google Play package fudge.block.puzzle.colorblast
Published by Golden Garuda Studios

Last updated 26 October 2026.

The short version

Block Puzzle Color Blast keeps your scores, settings and progress on your device. It shows no ads and sells nothing about you. Two things can leave your device: anonymous usage and crash data, sent to Google's Firebase (in Europe only if you say yes, and you can change your answer in Settings); and, if you use Google Play Games, your best score and achievements, kept in your Google account.

What the app stores on your device

One private file, holding only what the game needs to work:

  • your best score and your statistics: games played, total score, blocks placed, lines cleared, best combo, and how many days in a row you have played
  • the game you are part-way through, so you can carry on where you left off
  • your power-ups, your daily missions and their progress, your streak, and your badges and achievement progress
  • your settings: sound, vibration, colour theme, day or night, and whether usage and crash data are shared

That file is not copied to any backup or server. Android removes it when you uninstall Block Puzzle Color Blast, and deleting the app's data in Android Settings erases it immediately.

Usage and crash data

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and on any device whose region the app cannot tell, nothing is shared until you switch on Share usage data in Settings → Privacy & Terms. Switching it off again stops all further collection straight away. Everywhere else the data is shared, since it is anonymous and used only to fix and improve the game; if you would rather it were not, uninstalling the app stops it, and you can have what was collected deleted as described below. The app works out your region from your device's region setting and makes no network call to locate you.

While it is on, the app uses two Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics receives game events — a game started or ended with its score, a mission or achievement completed, a mission box opened, a power-up used, a theme chosen, the tutorial started. Firebase adds standard technical details: the app version, your device model and Android version, your approximate country (derived from your network address, which is not stored), and a random identifier for this installation of the app. We use this only to see how the game is played and to improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives a report if the app crashes: what the app was doing, the app version, your device model and Android version, and a random identifier for this installation. We use this only to fix bugs.

Neither contains your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. The app tells Firebase that consent for advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization is denied, so nothing collected is used for advertising. Firebase can read Android's advertising identifier on devices where it is available; with advertising consent denied it is not used for ads, and you can delete or reset it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

This data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms and kept no longer than our Firebase project's retention settings allow: usage data for at most 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. To have data already collected from your device deleted, see Delete your data or email us at the address below.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, Block Puzzle Color Blast uses it to:

  • post your best score to a leaderboard. Other players see your Play Games name and avatar next to your score, as with any Play Games leaderboard; and
  • record achievements, such as score milestones, combos and missions completed.

We do not receive your Google account details. This data is held by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, and you can manage or delete it in the Google Play Games app and in your Google account settings. If you do not use Play Games, nothing is sent.

Google Play updates

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and can download it for you. That is handled by Google Play under Google's own terms; we receive nothing from it.

Reading this policy in the app

The Privacy & Terms page in Settings shows copies of these documents that ship inside the app, so reading them there sends nothing.

Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — for the usage and crash data, Google Play Games and Google Play updates.
  • VIBRATE — for the small haptic taps when you place blocks and clear lines. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.
  • AD_ID — added by Firebase; see above. The app shows no ads.

If you email us

If you send us a message, we will see your email address and whatever you wrote, and we use it only to answer you. We keep it only as long as needed to do so.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent any information about you.
  • We do not show advertising, and nothing collected is used for advertising.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location through the app.

Children

Block Puzzle Color Blast is a puzzle game for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage and crash data contain no names or contact details. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If a future version of Block Puzzle Color Blast changes what it collects or sends — for example by adding ads — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.
